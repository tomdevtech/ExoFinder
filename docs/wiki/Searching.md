# Searching

The search finds planets and stars by their name, and the moons and dwarf planets of our solar system. It is in the bar at the top of every page, and in the middle of the start page.

## Find a planet or a star quickly

1. Click into the search field. It says **Search planets and stars**.
2. Type at least two letters of the name, for example `trappist`.
3. A list of suggestions opens below the field. Each line shows the name and, on the right, what it is: a star with its number of planets, a planet with its kind and its star, or a moon with the planet it orbits.
4. Click a suggestion. Its page opens.

![Suggestions while you type](images/search-suggestions.png)

**With the keyboard:** press the arrow keys **↓** and **↑** to move through the suggestions, then **Enter** to open the marked one. **Esc** closes the list.

## See all results

Sometimes you want to see everything that matches, not just the first suggestions.

1. Type the name or a part of it.
2. Press **Enter** without choosing a suggestion.
3. The search page opens with all planets and stars that match, up to 60 of them. Planets show their star next to their name.
4. Click a card to open its page.

![The search page with its results](images/search-results.png)

## List the planets of one kind

You can also look for a kind of planet instead of a name, for example all gas giants.

1. Open the search page: press **Enter** in the search field.
2. Below the heading are two rows of buttons. **Type** says how big a planet is: **Rocky planet**, **Super-Earth**, **Neptune-like planet** or **Gas giant**. **Climate** says how warm it is: **Frozen**, **Cold**, **Temperate**, **Hot** or **Scorching**.
3. Click a button. The page lists all planets of that kind, 60 per page, and says how many there are. The numbers at the bottom lead to the other pages.
4. Click one button in each row to combine them. **Rocky planet** with **Temperate** lists the planets most like Earth.
5. Type a name into the search field and press **Enter** to keep only the planets of that kind with this name.
6. Click a chosen button again to remove it. It is the one with the **✕**.

A list by kind holds planets only. Stars and moons have no kind, so they are left out. The [list of all planets](All-Planets) has the same buttons, and there you can also sort by year of discovery.

## Tips

- **Upper and lower case do not matter.** `kepler-22`, `Kepler-22` and `KEPLER-22` find the same.
- **A part of the name is enough.** `22 b` finds Kepler-22 b.
- **Typos are forgiven.** If no name contains what you typed, the search shows names that are similar.
- **Too many results?** Type more of the name. The page tells you when there are more matches than it shows.
- **Use the current name.** If a planet or a star was renamed, search for its new name. Old links to its page still work.
