Changes: scaled down and compressed again. The maps of the planets come in up to three sizes: small (512 pixels wide) for lists, large (2048) for the 3D model, and extra large (4096, `-xl`) for the 3D model zoomed in, where the source is that large. `planets/earth-relief-*` is put together from two maps of Solar System Scope: how far the ground leans east (red) and north (green), from its normal map, and the seas (blue), from its specular map. `planets/earth-clouds-flow.webp` is no picture of theirs but our own: the winds that keep the cloud map moving, towards east (red) and north (green), made by [scripts/earth-winds.ts](../../scripts/earth-winds.ts) from the lows that show in the cloud map and the usual winds along the latitudes. `planets/venus-*` is the map of the cloud tops, not of the surface. The maps of the moons, Ceres and Pluto were also turned so that longitude 0 lies in the middle; grey mosaics were tinted with the body's colours, exaggerated or false colours toned down, the brightness set after the body's albedo, and parts no spacecraft saw filled with a smooth continuation of the neighbouring tones ([moon-maps.ts](../../scripts/moon-maps.ts)). `planets/titan-*` shows Titan's haze, with the near-infrared map of its surface showing through faintly.

The files in `presets/` are not pictures but data: the base maps the exoplanets are drawn from. For rocky planets red is the height after erosion, green where water gathers (or the cracks of an ice crust), blue the craters; `<name>-relief` holds the slopes of the same ground, those of the height in its upper half and those of the craters in its lower half, each towards east (red) and north (green). For gas giants red are the belts and zones, green a second tracer for streaks, blue the storms. The `weather-*` maps are the clouds of rocky planets with seas: red is low cloud, green high cloud, blue thunderstorms. `<name>-flow` holds the winds of a banded or weather map, towards east (red) and north (green), which keep its clouds moving. The shader turns all of them into a planet with the values of that planet.
